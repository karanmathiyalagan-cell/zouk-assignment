Make Your Own Set: UX and AOV audit

The builder has the right bones: a box you fill slot by slot, a running total, and a nudge to add one more before paying. It is not ready for development yet. The tray total never matches the review screen, every step shows office bags whatever it asks for, and at 10–15% off the box is no better than the 15% banner at the top of the same screen. Fix those three and this feature can lift order value.

What already works

The slot tray shows the goal

Empty “+” slots next to filled thumbnails make the box feel unfinished. That pull toward completion is the main AOV engine here, so keep it.

Box of 3 is marked Most popular

A sensible default that puts Box of 4 in the anchor position. It just needs the savings beside it to do its job.

“Add more & save more” on review

On the Box of 2 and 3 reviews, naming the next tier (“Add 1 more product to get 15% off”) next to one-tap picks is the strongest upsell in the file.

An exit that doesn’t trap people

“Check out now” plus a confirm step lets someone leave with 2 or 3 items instead of abandoning. Fewer lost orders, as long as the dialog sells the upgrade (see A5).

Built from the PLP

The product card, filter bar and badges come from the Office Bags PLP, so the builder feels like the same site.

Two ways in

The entry sits under both Women and Sale with a “Save more” tag, which catches browsers and deal-seekers.

A · Offer and pricing (product)

BlockerA1

The tray total is wrong at almost every step

Box of 4 / Steps 2–4 (both states) · Box of 3 / Steps 2–3 · Box of 2 / Step 2 · all three review screens

The total doesn’t change when a product is added: “Step 2 · Product added” still reads ₹1,499 with two bags in the box. The struck-through price jumps between unrelated numbers (₹1,874, ₹2,998, ₹3,371), and the third bag adds only ₹299. None of the tray totals match the review screen. On Box of 2 the saving even drops at review, from ₹375 to ₹299.80.

Why it matters. The running total is the reason the tray exists. A shopper who sees ₹2,697 and then ₹4,796.80 one tap later reads it as a bait-and-switch, at the most expensive point in the funnel to lose her.

Fix

Write the pricing rule down once (see A3) and drive every number from it. The tray shows the total, one struck-through reference, and the % the box currently earns, and updates on every add or remove. Add a “Product added” frame for every step with real arithmetic so developers have a reference.

BlockerA2

The box is not a better deal than offers already on screen

Announcement bar on every builder frame · review screens · live zouk.co.in

Every builder screen carries “Flat 15% off on orders above ₹2,500”. Unless the box discount stacks with it, the builder is worse than ignoring it for two items and no better for three. On the live site, wallets run “Buy any 2 at ₹1,299 / ₹1,399” and Special Combos already sells fixed bag + sling sets at ₹2,249–₹2,549, so a deal-seeker has stronger offers one menu row away.

Why it matters. Shoppers compare. If the builder isn’t clearly the best deal, it gets ignored, or worse, it trains people that “build your own” means paying more. Support will also field “which offer applies?” questions.

Fix

Decide stacking first: does the banner or a coupon apply on top, and do BOGO items count toward a box? Then make every box size beat the banner. Two options: stack the box % on top of the banner, or switch to round box prices that match how Zouk already sells (“Any 3 for ₹3,499”, “Any 4 for ₹4,299”). Round prices also remove the ₹1,199.20-style paise (C3).

BlockerA3

Four different offer models in one file

Flow page overview · Box of 4 · With milestones · tray · review screens · confirm dialogs

  • Flow page (V2, 22 Oct): “No discounts, tiers or offer logic in this version.”
  • Milestones frame: free shipping at 2 items, free gift wrap at 3, 20% off at 4.
  • Tray: 20% off from the second item in a Box of 4 (₹600 saved on two bags).
  • Review screens: 10% / 15% / 20% by box size.

The exit dialog doesn’t say which rate applies if someone leaves with 2 of 4. And the “Free shipping” milestone is worth nothing: Zouk already ships free on every prepaid order (shipping policy). It only has value as a COD-fee waiver.

Fix

Pick one model and tie the reward to items in the box, not to the box chosen: 2 items earn tier 1, 3 earn tier 2, 4 earn tier 3. Box size then becomes a target, and leaving early drops you to a lower tier without any surprise. Swap “Free shipping” for something with real value: free gift wrap, free initials (the Flow page prices them at ₹149) or a waived COD fee.

HighA4

The savings ladder is hidden until the last screen

Menu / Women · Menu / Sale · step headers · tray

The menu says “Save more” but lists Box of 2, 3 and 4 with no numbers. Step headers and the tray never say what the box earns. 10% / 15% / 20% first appears on the review screen, after the size decision is made. The menu can’t sell the bigger box if it doesn’t show the reward.

Fix

Show the ladder where people choose, and keep it visible while they fill the box:

Box of 2 · 10% off
Box of 3 · 15% off · Most popular
Box of 4 · 20% off · Best value

Add a size switcher to the builder header (the Flow page had “Combo of 4 ▾”) so upgrading mid-way is one tap. Tray copy: “2 of 4 added · add 2 more for 20% off”.

HighA5

The exit dialog doesn’t say what leaving costs

Overlay / Confirm · 2 products, 3 products, 2 of 3 products

“Checkout with 2 of 4 products?” shows no price, no lost saving and no suggestion. “Add more products” just drops the shopper back into a grid. This is the moment of highest intent in the whole flow, and it is spent on a yes/no question.

Fix

Frame it as a loss, with numbers, and put two or three one-tap add-ons matched to her bag inside the sheet:

You’re 2 items away from 20% off
Check out now: ₹2,698 (save ₹300)
Complete your box: save ₹1,199
[ + Same-print wallet ₹1,299 ] [ + Pouch ₹699 ]
[ Complete my box ] [ Pay ₹2,698 for 2 ]

Prices in the copy are illustrative; wire them to the real rule from A3.

HighA6

Upgrade cards don’t show the real price of the next item

Box of 2 / Preview · Box of 3 / Preview · “Add more & save more”

Going from Box of 2 to Box of 3 moves the total from ₹2,698.20 to ₹3,822.45, so the third bag effectively costs ₹1,124 instead of ₹1,499. The recommendation cards show ₹1,499 with a ₹2,999 strike, which hides the best argument for upgrading. It is also unclear that tapping “+” turns a Box of 2 into a Box of 3.

Fix

Show the effective price on each card (“+₹1,124 in your box”) and retitle the rail “Upgrade to Box of 3 · 15% off on everything”. After the add, confirm with a toast: “Now a Box of 3. You save ₹675.”

MediumA7

Box of 4 review has nothing after the list

Box of 4 / Preview · Review your combo

Once the box is full there is no further offer. The Flow page already planned personalised initials (+₹149), and the Gift Shop sells gift-box combos, so the parts for a last add-on exist.

Fix

Add low-friction extras under the list: gift wrap toggle, initials on the main bag (+₹149), and “Start another box” for gifting. Keep them below the items and above the pay bar, one line each.

MediumA8

“Proceed to pay” skips the cart, and the cart already has 3 items

All review screens · header cart badge

The header shows 3 items in the cart. It’s unclear whether “Proceed to pay” checks out the box alone or the whole cart. The Flow page planned for box items to land in the cart as a group with “Edit combo”; the Final page jumps straight to payment. Returns aren’t covered either: if one item of a box comes back, how is the discount recalculated?

Fix

Add the box to the cart as a group (keeps cart offers working and lets people keep shopping, which helps AOV), with “Pay now” as the express option. State the partial-return rule in one line on the review screen.

B · Flow and interaction (UX)

BlockerB1

Every step shows office bags

Steps 2–4 in Box of 2, 3 and 4 · review screens

“Add a wallet”, “Add a pouch or organiser” and “Finish with a charm” all show ₹1,399–₹1,499 women’s office bags tagged “Fits 15″ Laptop” and “Top pick for office”. Step 1’s filter chips (“Bestseller”, “Up to 15″”) carry over to every step. The review ends with four office bags, which is not a set.

Why it matters for AOV. Order value comes from attaching cheaper, complementary items that need little thought: a wallet in the same print, a pouch, a charm. Asking someone to pick four ₹1,499 bags is a much harder sell than one bag plus three easy extras.

Fix

Give each step its own grid and filters: wallets (zip, bi-fold, card case), pouches and organisers, charms. Reset filters between steps. Even if the Final page is a mock, these frames will be read as the spec.

HighB2

“Same print first” isn’t delivered

Step 2 subtitle · Step 4 grid

After picking the Flomotif bag, Step 2’s first result is Mughal Art. Step 4 offers the Flomotif bag again, already in the box, with no “In your box” state, so duplicates are possible.

Fix

Pre-select a “Same print as your bag” chip, pin those items first with a small “Same print” tag, and mark anything already in the box. A matching set is Zouk’s real advantage here; make it the default.

HighB3

No size switch, no save, and the whole site is one tap away

Header on all builder frames · Scroll Area layers “Press Strip” and “Site Footer”

The Flow page specified a size chip, a “Leave your combo?” sheet that saves the box for 7 days, and a quick view. The Final page dropped all three. Meanwhile the builder keeps the full site header (menu, search, wishlist, cart) and the press strip and site footer under the grid. Each one is an exit with no way back to a half-built box.

Fix

Use a focused header: back arrow, “Box of 4 ▾” switcher, close with a save-draft sheet. Drop the footer inside the builder. Bring back “Continue your box (2)” in the menu for saved drafts.

HighB4

You can’t remove or swap from the tray

Box Tray component · product card “✓ ADDED” state

Filled slots are plain thumbnails with no tap action or remove control. The card switches to “✓ ADDED” with no undo. Editing only exists on the review screen. Empty slots are unlabelled, so the tray doesn’t say what comes next.

Fix

Tap a filled slot to get Swap / Remove / View. Let “Added ✓” toggle back. Label the slots (Bag, Wallet, Pouch, Charm) so the tray doubles as a progress map.

MediumB5

Previous and Next don’t say much

Box Tray, all steps

On Step 1, a disabled “Previous” takes half the bar. A disabled “Next” doesn’t say why. The Flow page had better labels: “Next: Wallet →” and “Pick a wallet to continue”.

Fix

Move back to the header arrow. Give the single primary button a specific label: “Pick a wallet to continue” while disabled, “Next: Pouch” when enabled, “Review box” on the last step.

MediumB6

Fixed bars take a third of the screen

Header (124px) + Box Tray (136px) on an 844px frame

260px of fixed bars, plus the step header and two filter rows, leave room for about one row of products on Step 1.

Fix

Hide the promo bar inside the builder and shrink the tray to a ~64px bar (slots + total + button) that expands on tap.

MediumB7

Frames disagree with each other

Step 1 vs Step 1 · Product added · Box of 4 · With milestones · review “+” circles

  • Step 1’s second card is Mughal Art in the empty state and FloLov once a product is added.
  • The milestones frame ticks three milestones with three items, shows a check over the second tray thumbnail, and disables Next.
  • The “+” circles between review rows look like buttons but are decoration (no hotspot). Remove them or make them insert an item.
Fix

One QA pass so each “added” frame is its empty frame plus exactly one change.

C · UI consistency (vs. the rest of the file and zouk.co.in)

HighC1

Savings are shown in red here and green on the PLP

Review pay bar · tray “You saved” pill · vs. 06 PLP / Women’s Office Bags (Web/Mobile Navigation page)

The builder writes “You are saving ₹1,199.20 on this combo.” in red and puts “You saved ₹375” in a maroon pill. The PLP in the same file uses green with a check: “You’re saving ₹799”. Red reads as an error or a price increase. “Saved” is also the wrong tense before payment.

Fix

Use the PLP’s green savings treatment everywhere and write “You save ₹1,199”.

MediumC2

Four button styles in one flow

Product card · Box Tray · review pay bar · PLP sticky bar

Cards use uppercase “ADD TO BAG” with a small radius. The tray uses sentence-case full pills (“Previous”, “Next”). The review uses a pill “Proceed to pay”. The PLP’s sticky bar uses uppercase “VIEW CART →”. The live site’s primary button has a 4px radius.

Fix

One primary and one secondary button spec for the builder, matched to the site’s current button.

MediumC3

Prices with paise, and three different reference prices

Review screens · tray · product cards

Review prices read ₹1,199.20, ₹4,796.80, ₹1,274.15. The live site and the rest of this file use whole rupees. The struck price is MRP (₹2,999) on cards, ₹1,874 in the tray and the sale price (₹1,499) on review, so the same bag shows three “original” prices.

Fix

Round to whole rupees. Keep MRP as the one struck reference (the site standard: “Regular price ₹2,499 · 51% OFF”) and show the box discount as its own line: “Box of 4 discount −₹1,199”.

MediumC4

Badges, chips and titles drift

  • “Limited stoc” is clipped on several cards.
  • “Price drop” is sentence case here and “PRICE DROP” on the PLP frame.
  • The “STEP 1 OF 4” chip has wider letter-spacing in the “Product added” frames than in the empty ones, which suggests a detached instance.
  • The review title is centred; every other screen title is left-aligned.
  • Product names truncate on the Box of 2 and 3 reviews but wrap on Box of 4.
Fix

Re-link the badge and step-chip instances to their components, use auto-width badges, and left-align the review title.

MediumC5

The exit dialog breaks the mobile pattern

Overlay / Confirm frames (720px tall vs. 844px elsewhere)

A centred modal on an opaque near-black backdrop, with no close control. The Flow page used bottom sheets for the same kind of decision, which suits one-handed use.

Fix

Bottom sheet over a translucent scrim with a close button, on an 844px frame like the rest.

MediumC6

Six names for one feature

Page: “Make Your Own Set”. Menu: “Build your own combo”. Flow page: “Make your own combo”. Screens: “Box of 4”. Review: “Review your combo”. And the Sale menu already has “Combos & Sets” for fixed combos, which invites confusion.

Fix

Pick one name and one noun, used everywhere from the menu to the order confirmation. “Build your box” works well with “Box of 3”, and stays clear of “Combos & Sets”.

MediumC7

Stale details from the live site

The app banner reads “4.5 ★ | 5L+ Downloads”; the live site says “4.6 ⭐ | 10L+ Downloads”. The FloMotif Women’s Office Bag is ₹1,214 (MRP ₹2,499) live versus ₹1,499 (₹2,999) in the file. Fine for a mock, but update before these screens go into a spec or a pitch.

AOV plan

What to do, in order

Ranked by how much each change should move order value against the effort to ship it. Items 1–3 are prerequisites; the rest add upside.

  1. One pricing rule, correct numbers everywhere

    Reward by items in the box, round rupees, MRP as the only struck price, tray updates on every change.

    A1 · A3 · C3BlockerEffort M

Measurement

How to tell if it works

Primary: AOV of orders containing a box vs. site AOV, and items per order.

Funnel: menu tap → first pick → each step → review → paid. Box completion rate (full box vs. early exit). Share of exit sheets that end in “add more”. Upgrade rate from review cards.

Guardrails: builder conversion rate, discount cost as a share of incremental revenue, shift away from BOGO and Special Combos, return rate on box items.

Tests worth running

Four A/B tests

  • Savings ladder shown in the menu vs. hidden.
  • Default size Box of 3 vs. Box of 4.
  • Loss-framed exit sheet with add-ons vs. the current dialog.
  • Round box prices (“Any 3 for ₹3,499”) vs. % off.

Tag box items with a box ID as a cart attribute so these can be measured; the Flow page already lists this as an open question.

For the business

Open questions to settle before design continues

  • Does the box discount stack with the sitewide banner, coupons or app offers?
  • Can BOGO or Special Combo items go into a box, and at which price?
  • Is the reward set by the box chosen or by the items in it?
  • What happens to the discount when one item from a box is returned or exchanged?
  • Can a box hold two bags, or is it strictly one bag plus accessories?
  • Does “Proceed to pay” include items already in the cart?
